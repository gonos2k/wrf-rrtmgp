This package retains six canonical UDM27 frozen-mode1 column anchors with successful LW and SW strict replays, plus two historical audit sites. It extends the replay contract validated in [PR39](https://github.com/gonos2k/wrf-rrtmgp/pull/39), based on commit `22ab474286c7b808d6f81e08ce457ddf6ba8e9cb`. The evidence covers one-minute runs from one 12-hour restart checkpoint. It does not establish domain radiation accuracy, climatological coverage, or a longer forecast outcome.

[canonical-summary.md](canonical-summary.md) lists the six canonical states: CF0 rain, material CF0 snow, two cloud-ice clipping cases, clear sky, and a daylight unclipped liquid cloud. [index.json](index.json) records actual eligibility, native phase masses, adapter mapping, captured column seeds, and exact original receipt paths and hashes. The two historical audits retain the original snow-proxy selection and the nighttime control at **i=22,j=37**. Total evidence comprises eight WRF anchors: seven full LW/SW anchors and one LW-only nighttime anchor, with eight LW and seven SW reference calls. No nighttime SW call was attempted.

Checkpoint ranks are selection proxies. The canonical snow replacement captured 5.62248349 g/m² of omitted CF0 snow, with six native layers above 0.001 g/m². The historical snow selection captured approximately 2.99e-12 g/m² despite its 203 g/m² checkpoint proxy, so its intended material-snow criterion remains unmet. The canonical liquid control captured 3.52704612 g/m² of active liquid, an explicit radius of 5.26817846 µm, no active ice, and COSZEN=0.41168997. It establishes liquid-cloud coverage, without an unclipped active-ice claim. Eligible ice clipping and realized cloud masks are recorded separately.

Native grid masses use corrected mixing ratio × native dry-layer mass × 1000. Rain/snow adapter paths divide by positive cloud fraction and become zero at CF0. Frozen graupel/hail mode1 retains native GRID=RADIATION=adapter mass, with exactly zero omitted and padded paths. Actual CF0 grid masses for all six hydrometeor phases remain separate from in-cloud adapter paths. Native radius fields are meters; adapter REL/REI/RES are micrometers after the recorded source or background-fallback mapping. Liquid uses effective radius REL and lookup bounds 2.5–21.5 µm. Cloud ice uses requested diameter 2×REI and bounds 10–180 µm; DI_USED records clipping. Frozen graupel/hail uses its distinct slope/table contract. These mass paths are not optical-depth bounds.

Every retained history is byte-identical to SHA `7a76a430f1d37ba88ee2692c5cb076a271f3d525a1190b5cd824df3790225025`: geometry 289×189×39, MP27/RA37/37, all 210 numeric arrays finite and unmasked in raw and decoded reads.
