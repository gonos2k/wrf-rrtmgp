# UDM precipitation optics: bounded source inventory

**Finding:** the inspected public CCPP source provides one generic snow-plus-graupel path, but it supplies only a snow effective radius for that path. That establishes a precedent for a host-specific approximation, not a verified optical equivalence for UDM graupel. I found no public CCPP/RRTMGP hail field or hail coefficient mapping for UDM `qh`.

At WRF commit [`bea2284`](https://github.com/gonos2k/wrf-rrtmgp/tree/bea2284dbb4d40fcdea691f49a0f9d91fccde93c), UDM carries `qs`, `qg`, and `qh` as kg/kg mixing ratios ([Registry fields](https://github.com/gonos2k/wrf-rrtmgp/blob/bea2284dbb4d40fcdea691f49a0f9d91fccde93c/WRF/Registry/Registry.EM_COMMON#L468-L473), UDM package [registration](https://github.com/gonos2k/wrf-rrtmgp/blob/bea2284dbb4d40fcdea691f49a0f9d91fccde93c/WRF/Registry/Registry.EM_COMMON#L3093)). The microphysics uses separate snow, graupel, and hail PSD slopes and constants ([source](https://github.com/gonos2k/wrf-rrtmgp/blob/bea2284dbb4d40fcdea691f49a0f9d91fccde93c/WRF/phys/module_mp_udm.F#L14-L44), [graupel](https://github.com/gonos2k/wrf-rrtmgp/blob/bea2284dbb4d40fcdea691f49a0f9d91fccde93c/WRF/phys/module_mp_udm.F#L2863-L2907), [hail](https://github.com/gonos2k/wrf-rrtmgp/blob/bea2284dbb4d40fcdea691f49a0f9d91fccde93c/WRF/phys/module_mp_udm.F#L2912-L2956)). But `udm_mp_effective_radius` accepts only `qs` for snow size, derives it from `qs`, density, temperature-dependent snow intercept, and clips its radius to 25–999 µm; it has no `qg` or `qh` input ([source](https://github.com/gonos2k/wrf-rrtmgp/blob/bea2284dbb4d40fcdea691f49a0f9d91fccde93c/WRF/phys/module_mp_udm.F#L3618-L3716)). That radius therefore does not define a `qs+qg` mixture size or hail size.

The pinned UFS CCPP source is `NCAR/ccpp-physics@3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860`, the submodule pin in `NOAA-EMC/ufsatm@6f461419f091c109d18b826ab88595da00ab336c`. In its generic `cloud_mp_uni`, slot 4 is `i_cldsnow + i_cldgrpl`; when cloud fraction exceeds 0.001, the path is computed as mixing ratio times `1e5/g` times pressure thickness in hPa, yielding grid-mean g/m² without division by cloud fraction. With supplied effective radii, the slot receives `effrin_cldsnow`, with no separate graupel radius ([pinned source lines 576–640](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/Interstitials/UFS_SCM_NEPTUNE/GFS_rrtmgp_cloud_mp.F90#L576-L640)). This is not universal across CCPP hosts: the same file's Thompson-specific mapper sets the slot from snow alone ([lines 657–739](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/Interstitials/UFS_SCM_NEPTUNE/GFS_rrtmgp_cloud_mp.F90#L657-L739)). The pinned SW/LW RRTMGP driver APIs carry cloud liquid/ice and rain/snow inputs but no hail input or hail optics ([SW](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/Radiation/RRTMGP/rrtmgp_sw_main.F90#L83-L142), [LW](https://github.com/NCAR/ccpp-physics/blob/3e6660c6df54e95a0871e990c2294dd397ae3860/physics/Radiation/RRTMGP/rrtmgp_lw_main.F90#L86-L140)).

The current UDM adapter makes its policy explicit: it records graupel in the omitted-path diagnostic and sets its radiative path to zero; positive hail path raises `RRTMGP_INPUT_UDM_HAIL_OPTICS_UNSUPPORTED` ([input builder](https://github.com/gonos2k/wrf-rrtmgp/blob/bea2284dbb4d40fcdea691f49a0f9d91fccde93c/WRF/phys/module_ra_rrtmgp_input.F#L38-L65), [SW gate](https://github.com/gonos2k/wrf-rrtmgp/blob/bea2284dbb4d40fcdea691f49a0f9d91fccde93c/WRF/phys/module_ra_rrtmg_sw.F#L11350-L11367)). Those choices avoid silently labeling unsupported species as snow or hail optics.

There are research precedents, but neither is a validated drop-in for this path. Hill et al. calculate and fit rain/graupel/hail extinction, single-scattering albedo, and asymmetry using Mie theory for SOCRATES bands ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1029/2018MS001415)); Donovan et al. describe UV–IR Mie optics on equivalent effective-radius spheres for graupel/hail and note phase-function limitations ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.5194/amt-16-5327-2023)). These require their own PSD, material/shape assumptions, and band mapping. They do not establish UDM `qh` coefficients for RRTMGP.

To support hail credibly, the implementation would still need a declared PSD-to-effective-size relationship tied to UDM `qh`, `N0h`, density, and air density; particle shape and material/refractive-index assumptions (including any melting or liquid coating); and spectrally integrated extinction, SSA, and phase/asymmetry properties mapped to RRTMGP bands and independently validated. Applying snow optics to hail, or applying the generic CCPP snow-plus-graupel path with the UDM snow-only radius, would be a new approximation requiring a declared physical contract and independent validation.
