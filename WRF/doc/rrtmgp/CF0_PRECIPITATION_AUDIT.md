# Held-state CF0 precipitation audit

The standalone [test reference](../../test/rrtmgp/reference_column.f90) accepts an optional sixth argument containing a `RRTMGP_CF0_PRECIP_AUDIT_V1` sidecar. This is a test-only contribution experiment. Production physics, cloud fraction and the CF0 rain/snow occurrence contract are unchanged. Calls without a sidecar retain the original multi-column interface; audit sidecars support one column and V8/V9 inputs only.

```text
reference_column DATA_DIR INPUT OUTPUT 1 '' SIDECAR
```

The sidecar declares phase, one column, native depth, one rain/snow species, occurrence exactly one and `PATH_UNITS_G_M2`. It contains native-sized `AUDIT_RWP_GRID` and `AUDIT_SWP_GRID` arrays. Paths must be finite and nonnegative, the other species zero, and engine padding zero. The audit reader requires finite CF in [0,1] and positive paths only where CF is exactly zero. An absent or all-zero sidecar skips extra optical allocation, helper calls, increments and audit output records. This skip is the intended default-preservation contract; solver equality must be measured separately.

The [Python validator](../../test/rrtmgp/cf0_precip_sidecar.py) authenticates positive sidecar paths against the paired captured `RWP_OMITTED` or `SWP_OMITTED` record. Snow requires native source-radius capability, non-background source radius and matching prepared `RES`, including the documented float32 serialization tolerance. `--zero-control` instead requires both sidecar paths to be zero. The caller must pin the input/raw provenance and loader closure. The Python tests reject malformed units, dimensions, nonfinite/negative paths, invalid CF, species mixtures, wrong occurrence, trailing data, changed omitted mass and unsuitable snow radii. They do not exercise or prove compiled Fortran error paths.

Positive extra precipitation is inserted after sampled cloud optics and before frozen optics/RTE, at occurrence one. It is separate from accepted precipitation and does not change CF or the mask. `PREPARED_*` remains baseline cloud-plus-accepted-precipitation optics. Original clear-sky, gas/frozen and ordinary pre-delta direct diagnostics retain their baseline meaning; normal all-sky total optics, flux and heating may change. SW emits separate extra precipitation optics and `AUDIT_DIRECT_PREDELTA`, which adds the audit raw extinction to the baseline direct path. Unchanged ordinary pre-delta diagnostics are not the intervention's direct flux.

[The retained evidence](../../../validation/rrtmgp37/cf0-precipitation-held-audit/README.md) separates the historical held-capture campaign from the current compiled-reader test. Exactly eight historical reference calls passed: four zero controls were byte-identical to their saved original double oracles, and four positive rain/snow cases passed the declared output contract and independent readback. Their conditional flux/heating deltas are retained without claiming occurrence correctness or physical accuracy.

Historical source `939981fd` uses focused-v2 archives and differs from the current private-scalar backend. Current source `93214328` adds defensive CF checks and was separately compiled/tested. Its synthetic three-layer reader test records 14 reference processes (10 successful, four rejected before output) plus two fixture programs making three adapter RTE calls. It tests the current reader; it is not a new captured WRF column or forecast proof. Python parser controls are distinct from these compiled controls.

Original uncompiled/NOT_EXECUTED preparation and all initial failed generations remain preserved alongside later results. The portable retained-artifact verifier checks text hashes and compact contracts, not external numerical recomputation. Workspace launcher/analysis copies are nonportable provenance snapshots. Production CF0 occurrence remains unchanged and scientifically open; neither these sensitivities nor synthetic tests establish domain-average response, an independent optical oracle or LUT clipping accuracy.
